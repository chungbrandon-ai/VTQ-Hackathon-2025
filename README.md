# VTQ Hackathon 2025

Welcome to the VTQ Hackathon 2025! Here you'll find the official schedule, important notes, and resources for the event.

---

## 📅 Weekend Itinerary

### Saturday, November 8th

- **9:00am – 10:00am:** Registration and check-in
- **10:00am – 11:00am:** Welcome session and Intro to Qiskit & Quantum by Samantha Barron (VT alumna at IBM Research)
- **11:00am:** Hacking begins
- **12:30pm:** Boxed lunch provided (grab-and-go)
- **All Day:** Light snacks and coffee available  
  *Participants are responsible for making their own dinner arrangements. You are encouraged to bring your own snacks and drinks.*

### Sunday, November 9th

- **11:00am:** Hacking ends
- **11:00am – 2:00pm:** Break for participants (judges deliberate)
- **2:00pm – 2:45pm:** Awards & closing session

---

## 💤 Overnight Accommodation

- The hacking session runs overnight—participants may enter or leave the venue as they wish.
- You may bring a sleeping bag. If you don't have one, you can rent one at Venture Out.

---

## ⚡ Venue & Equipment

- Plug/power strip availability is limited. Please bring your own power strip if possible.
- You must bring your own laptop.
- Snacks and coffee are provided. Don't forget to arrange your own dinner!

---

## 🧑‍💻 Project Details

- The competition problems will involve using **Qiskit**, IBM's SDK for quantum computing.
- An opening lecture on quantum computing and Qiskit 101 will be presented by Samantha Barron (IBM Research, VT alumna).

---

## 🍽️ Food

- **Saturday:** Boxed lunch provided; snacks and coffee throughout. Dinner arrangements are up to participants.
- **Sunday:** Light snacks during the break before closing session.
